_bio

pedro mileu is a Portuguese sound artist based in Berlin. His practice spans sound installation, electroacoustic composition and performance, and has more recently extended into multimedia contexts.

Working with sound as physical matter and as a relational medium, mileu develops works that explore materiality, feedback systems, psychoacoustic phenomena, and the interplay between sound, space and objects.
