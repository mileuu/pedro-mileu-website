to remain here

2026 — sound installation — UdK Rundgang 2026

_about

Vertical wooden forms stand throughout the room as mechanical agents strike their surfaces. Each impact releases a distinct tone, shaping dispersed rhythms within the space.

A biological gesture persists in synthetic form, enduring without demand, striking to communicate, sense, and sustain presence within an habitat that no longer grows.
