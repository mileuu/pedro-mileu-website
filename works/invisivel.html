Invisível

sound installation

Invisível explores the hidden presence of electromagnetic fields and their interplay with human technology.

Two wide-band receivers, connected to copper wires stretched across the room, act as sensors for the hidden activity of electromagnetic radiation. The signals they capture translate the invisible electromagnetic fields in this space, emitted from electronic devices, electrical wiring, and the environment itself.

This installation sounds different in every room, shaped by the electromagnetic fields of the space. What you hear is always unique to the moment and environment. The wires respond to human touch, creating short circuits between the system.
